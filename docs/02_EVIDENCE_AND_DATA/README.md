# 2 — What evidence do we actually have?

**Separate three things:** an available document, a verified statement in that document, and an actual local observation. They are not interchangeable.

## Available or located
- **UF/IFAS AE069:** authoritative general explanation of wet-bulb cooling and air warming downstream from the pad. It is not an Al-Shahaniya weather record.
- **Mahmood et al., Al-Shahaniya semi-closed greenhouse:** identified as a Qatar-specific study; the source status in different ledgers conflicts. Numeric claims require rechecking the original exact pages and measured-vs-modeled identity.
- **Ghani et al., evaporatively cooled greenhouse study:** a named PDF is present **in the uploaded local archive**, not established as an indexed verified source in live GitHub. Inspect the original before promoting its passages.
- **Regional/economic/regulatory references:** conditional research context, not site engineering evidence. Shannak–Kafi identity is unresolved; FAO remains excluded from the active retrieval scope pending an explicit change decision.
- **QMD/ERA5 weather:** access routes identified; no verified historical Qatar case data acquired for a design or crop-zone result.
- **Al-Shahaniya JSON described as “VERIFIED_FIELD_READINGS”: rejected as verified data** because no original sensor readings, calibration, source hash or custodian authorization came with it.

## What is missing
For soil ML: paired laboratory observations and imagery. For full greenhouse performance: actual site, sensor and mechanical data; crop-zone conditions; weather, radiation, geometry, ventilation/recirculation and water/energy inputs. For LLM evaluation: a locked, independently checked answer key and comparable original model-run receipts.

**Next step:** take one required claim and open its original source at the exact section/table. Record units, site/time, measured versus predicted, what it supports and what it cannot support. Leave the rest conditional.

**Do not upload entire third-party papers or a private intern ZIP into public GitHub.** Keep local copies in institution-approved storage with immutable original hashes and a rights ledger; only source IDs, locators and permitted short descriptions go here.
