# Greenhouse feasibility — minimum evidence request package

**Date:** 2026-11-07  
**Scope:** Proposed commercial tomato greenhouse in Qatar. **Pre-design only; not construction drawings or procurement approval.**  
**Evidence baseline:** **0 of 6 accepted for this proposed site**, pending receipt and review. This means none of the six site-specific evidence packages has been accepted here; it does **not** mean there is no existing research or supporting literature.

## Decision boundary

Proceed from feasibility screening to concept comparison only after a preliminary crop plan, HVAC/cooling feasibility calculation, water and utility assessments, costed alternative concept comparison, and preliminary risk register are supported by evidence. Do not approve irreversible construction spending or equipment capacities before qualified review. The published semi-closed Al-Shahaniya tomato greenhouse is a *reference case*, not a transferable construction design; national crop/import statistics are not site measurements.

### Acceptance-state rules

- **Accepted:** Verifiable document, measured dataset, lab result, utility confirmation or supplier quote satisfies the stated criterion and has been reviewed.
- **Missing:** Evidence not supplied or not verified.
- **Assumed:** Screening-only assumption with explicit source, range, uncertainty, sensitivity check and verification action. **Assumed is not Accepted.**
- **Not applicable:** Documented configuration-specific justification, reviewed by relevant specialist.
- Do not record generic estimates or unpriced budgets as accepted proof. Do not give final fan sizes, cooling kW, wiring/breaker sizes, pumps, emitters or water requirements without necessary design inputs.

## Minimum output/dependency matrix

| Output | Input dependencies | Required reviewer |
|---|---|---|
| Preliminary crop plan | E05 | Agronomist and commercial operator |
| Preliminary HVAC concept/load feasibility | E02, E05, concept information from E06; site/enclosure from E01 as applicable | Greenhouse HVAC engineer |
| Water and electric supply sufficiency | E03, E04 plus preliminary equipment and crop demands | Irrigation/water and electrical engineers |
| Two-concept techno-economic comparison | E01–E06, with assumptions marked | Project engineer and cost consultant |
| Risk register and gate decision | E01–E06 | Project sponsor and competent multidisciplinary team |

**Order:** Verify site, crop and seasonal climate -> develop competing greenhouse concepts -> calculate preliminary cooling/water/electricity requirements -> seek comparable supplier pricing -> test economic downside -> expert gate review -> only then detail engineering and procurement.
