## Read this before interpreting any work
- **OBSERVED:** a connector-read GitHub file or email exists. A document's existence does not validate its scientific assertions.
- **REPORTED:** author or earlier checkpoint reported a test or observation; independent rerun not established.
- **PREPARED:** source collection, protocols, prompt bank, scaffolds, demonstrations or plans; useful but not completed experiments.
- **BLOCKED:** attempted exercise did not satisfy evidence/calculation requirements.
- **AWAITING REVIEW:** supervisor acceptance/approval absent.
- **NOT DONE:** no evidence of execution.
- **UNKNOWN:** records cannot establish it. Do not silently change unknown to completed.
- **RESTRICTED:** internal correspondence, original agronomy book and private records should not be copied into public documentation. Use non-sensitive references only.
