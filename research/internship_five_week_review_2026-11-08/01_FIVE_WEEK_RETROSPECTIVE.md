# Five reporting weeks | advanced preparation → scientific fundamentals → bounded evidence

This is an accurate reframing, not retroactive completion. **Early advanced preparation was real and useful**: source discovery, scope design, data schema, protocols and offline systems provided reusable infrastructure. The subsequent fundamental exercises revealed what those systems did **not** validate. Growth means making these boundaries explicit.

Keep it archived. Do not build more until the pre-RAG gate answers whether retrieval is warranted.
