## Reference/recommendation strategy
A letter or reference is **not owed** by preparation or a presentation. Protect the relationship by (a) arriving ready, (b) acknowledging earlier meeting failure without disguising it, (c) completing a supervisor-agreed scientific receipt, (d) obtaining specific written feedback and incorporating it, (e) requesting an assessment of documented work only after a witnessed demonstration. Do not ask them to endorse model performance, agronomic expertise or attendance not verified.

## Proof threshold
A reference-ready packet should contain: dated work ledger; public-safe repo commits; raw reproducible runs with metadata; source/location checks and negative controls; 1+ independently reconstructed calculation; supervisor-reviewed evidence and corrections; short statement of limitations; consistent meeting/timesheet records. If any missing, label it pending.
